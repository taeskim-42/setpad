AI help (DeepSeek; Upstage only tells meal from exercise) and how to turn it off
- AI help is on by default. When an AI feature is used, what is sent (the text you type, meal photos with location and capture details removed, and the exercise names in your log) goes through our server gym.darak.studio to DeepSeek's AI; a line typed in the exercise-name field goes to Upstage's AI only to tell a meal from an exercise. Our server stores neither; the privacy policy describes this.
- To turn it off: on the list screen tap the gear icon › Settings › Recording › "AI help". While it is off, nothing is sent to the AI.
- Off never blocks the app: typed lines and "500kcal" still count, search works, and routines are built on the phone.
- Body composition sheet (optional): Settings › My body › "Take photo". The photo goes via our server to DeepSeek only to read the printed numbers and is not stored; values are checked before saving and stay on the phone.
- Workout import (optional): Settings › Recording › "Import workout history". Text or screenshots go via our server to DeepSeek only to read sets, are not stored, and nothing is saved before "Save all".
- Health data read from Apple Health (active energy, resting energy, heart rate) is never sent to our server or to the AI. Resting energy adds to calories used; without it the app estimates it from Settings › My body (kept on the phone).

How to try the AI features (signed out)
1. Launch the app; today's record opens. Tap the exercise-name field at the bottom.
2. One-line setup: type "bench 80kg 5x5" and press Return. The exercise appears right away with its goal under the title; tap "…" on the card to see the chips (80 kg, 5 sets of 5) and change a value in place. Then type "80 5" to log a set.
3. Meal from text: in the same field type "lunch burrito" and press Return. It is logged as a meal ("Logged as a meal"; "Make it exercise" undoes it). A review sheet then shows the food, amount and calories; fix the amount if needed and tap Done — nothing is saved before this.
4. Meal photo: tap the fork-and-knife button at the left of the bar above the keyboard, choose Camera or From library, and pick a food photo. Same review sheet as step 3.
5. Record question: go back to "All Workouts", type "best bench press" in the search field and press Return, then tap "Yes" to confirm. The table is computed on the phone from the saved sets; the AI only turns the question into a query.
6. Today's routine: in the same search field type "today's routine" (or "오늘 루틴 짜줘") and press Return. It is built on the phone from the user's past sessions (weights copied, never invented); tap "Start".
7. Settings › My body › Training goal: calorie and macro targets (reference, not medical advice); without a watch, exercise calories are marked "estimate".

What plates are
- Plates are the allowance for AI record questions (step 5), about 1–2 per question by tokens used. They are not sold on their own, not transferable, and have no cash value.
- A new device gets 3 plates, a signed-in account 5, and 1 more on each day the user logs 10 sets. The Pro subscription (auto-renewable) tops plates up to 300 each month and raises input help from 10 to 20 a day.
- Input help (one-line setup, meal estimates, workout import) does not use plates: 10 a day free.
- Logging, timers, Health, working out together and gyms are free.

Pro (Settings › Pro; Monthly/Yearly, no trial) is bought and restored without signing in. Optional sign-in later moves it to the account for the user's other devices (5.1.1(v)).

Features that need sign-in (optional to review): Together (up to 6 people edit one record live) and gym connection. Trainer mode shows only for gym staff accounts.
